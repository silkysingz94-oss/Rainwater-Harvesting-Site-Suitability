METHODOLOGY

Candidate points are generated inside selected Enumeration Area polygons. Elevation values are retrieved from the Open-Meteo elevation service, which uses Copernicus DEM GLO-90.

Rainfall is calculated from annual sums of daily precipitation over the selected reference period using the Open-Meteo historical archive.

Slope and valley position are screening proxies derived from the sampled elevation surface. They are not equivalent to a full-resolution DEM terrain analysis.

Settlement proximity and mapped-waterway proximity are derived from OpenStreetMap features. OpenStreetMap coverage may be incomplete.

The waterway-distance criterion is not a verified inventory of drinking-water access or existing water infrastructure.

This is a preliminary suitability screening model. It does not calculate reservoir capacity, runoff volume, infiltration, groundwater recharge or engineering feasibility.
